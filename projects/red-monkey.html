Reconstructing a parkour gym as an interactive digital environment — from photographic acquisition to a navigable 3D model.

CAPTURE / RECONSTRUCTION / OPTIMIZATION / DELIVERY
THE PROJECT / 001

Making a real space explorable.

Red Monkey is a parkour gym captured and reconstructed through photogrammetry. The goal was to create a digital representation of the physical space that visitors could explore online.

The workflow brought together photographic acquisition, RealityCapture reconstruction, 3D optimization and interactive publication on Sketchfab, with the resulting model embedded in the gym's website.
